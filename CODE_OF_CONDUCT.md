# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be an open and welcoming place. Everyone who takes
part should be free from harassment. This applies to all people, whatever
their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of
experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps create a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different points of view and experiences
- Accepting helpful criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy to other people in the community

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or images, and unwanted sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment in public or in private
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission
- Other behaviour that would not be appropriate at work

## Our responsibilities

The maintainer explains the standards of acceptable behaviour. The maintainer
will take fair action when someone behaves in an unacceptable way. The
maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the project's spaces. This includes the
repository, its issues, and its pull requests. It also applies in public
spaces when a person represents the project.

## Reporting a problem

If you see behaviour that is abusive or harassing, please report it by email
to **karansingh25822@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated. The maintainer will respond in a way that fits the situation,
and will keep the reporter's identity private.
